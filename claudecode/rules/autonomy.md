# Autonomy

**Commit before dispatching work you will not step through — a subagent, a
workflow, a long script. When it comes back wrong, go back to that commit and
run it again; do not patch what came back.** ★Patching builds on a state nobody
can account for, and the next mistake will be harder to spot than this one.

★**Keep the failed attempt on a branch, and read it before re-running.** What
went wrong is the one thing the second attempt has that the first did not.
⚠️ Read it; do not continue from it.
