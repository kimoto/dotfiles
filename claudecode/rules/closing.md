# Closing

**Close a session when its work is done.** ★A judgement that needs time to pass
gets a fresh session scheduled for that date, with a prompt that stands alone —
then close. ⚠️ `send_later`, `CronCreate` and `/loop` resume or die with this
session, so they do not count. How: `wrapup` skill, step 3.

**Open at most three new issues per repo in one session.** Past two, the rest
become a checklist in the third. ★A dozen issues split from one session's
leftovers bury the ones that matter, and nobody triages them one by one; a
checklist is read in one sitting. A skill that names its own issue rule keeps
it.
