# Writing

**A human reads slowly, and every word costs them — past some length, it simply goes unread.** Producing text is free; the cost lands entirely on the reader.

**The opening carries the whole shape** — cause and trigger first, mechanism after and on request. It is the only part guaranteed to be read.

⚠️ **Use the established term — Japanese if one is in common use, otherwise the English word as is. Never coin an image for it.** A word the reader has to decode is wrong even when it is apt, and it spreads into docs, test names and the next session's writing. 実時間 or wall time, not 壁時計; tail latency, not 裾.
