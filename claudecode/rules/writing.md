# Writing

**A human reads slowly, and every word costs them — past some length, it simply goes unread.** Producing text is free; the cost lands entirely on the reader.

**The opening carries the whole shape** — cause and trigger first, mechanism after and on request. It is the only part guaranteed to be read.

⚠️ **Use plain words and short sentences, in any language.** For technical things, use the established term — Japanese if one is in common use, otherwise the English word as is — never a coined one: 実時間 or wall time, not 壁時計.
